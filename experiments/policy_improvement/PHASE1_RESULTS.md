### 5. Partial-denoising progression (NFE32)

The most informative figure of this phase. Columns = integration progress
(initial noise / 25% / 50% / 75% / final); rows = trajectory tokens, with row 4
labelled **GOAL CONDITION**, not `t=4`.

- **t=0 and GOAL rows are clean at every column** — conditioning is correctly
  re-imposed after every solver step, exactly as the code claims.
- **Predicted rows (t=1,2,3) stay visibly incoherent through 75%** — smeared
  white/grey structures — and snap to clean cubes only in the final segment.

**Answer to "when does recognizable structure appear?": very late, and abruptly.**
The states are **not** already coherent at the intermediate integration point;
most of the visible structure forms in the last quarter of integration. This is
a concrete, testable motivation for the §13 separate-schedule direction — state
and action almost certainly do not need identical time mappings — but it is an
observation about *integration progress*, not yet evidence about control.

## 12. Proposed λ screen — derived from measurement, not from equalizing

Objective for screening arms:

```
L = λ_state · L_state + λ_action · L_action
```

with `L_state`, `L_action` the **independently normalized masked means already
computed in code** (`flow_matching.py:293-296`). `λ_state = 1` fixed throughout.

Measured current effective weighting: the action block contributes
**0.879%** of the weighted numerator (`42 × 0.06981` against
`42 × 0.06981 + 1440 × 0.22972`).

| arm | λ_state | λ_action | first-action weight | terminal mask | weighted action share | what it changes |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|--:|---|
| **A** baseline | 1 | 1 | 10 | **no** | 0.879% | current objective, unchanged |
| **B** terminal-action masking | 1 | 1 | 10 | **yes** | 0.879%† | removes the zero-target supervision only (§2 ablation) |
| **C** moderate | 1 | **2** | 10 | yes | **1.74%** | doubles action emphasis relative to current |
| **D** stronger | 1 | **5** | 10 | yes | **4.24%** | ~5× action emphasis; still far from parity |

† B's share is nominally unchanged because masking removes 3 of 42 action weight
units *and* the corresponding loss term; its effect is on *what is supervised*,
not on the ratio.

**These multipliers are relative to the current implemented objective**, chosen as
a small ×2/×5 sweep around it. They are explicitly **not** chosen to equalize
`λ_action L_action ≈ λ_state L_state` — parity would need λ_action ≈ 112, two
orders of magnitude away, which §9 forbids and which nothing motivates. The
first-action weight of 10 is **held fixed at 10 in every arm** so that λ_action
and the terminal mask remain attributable (§10).

  change**, `action_weight=10` retained.
