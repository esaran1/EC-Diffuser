## 1. Problem

Diffusion/flow policies "denoise from scratch," needing many iterative steps and
creating latency that blocks real-time control. Framing question, quoted:

> "Do robotic policies truly need to be generated by sampling from random noise?"

## 2. Algorithm

Baseline flow matching with `p_0 = N(0,I)` and OT path `x_τ = (1−τ)x_0 + τx_1`
— **algebraically identical to our interpolant**, with `τ` for our `t`.

A2A **replaces the source**. With history `a_≤t`, images `I_≤t`, future `a_{>t}`:

```
z_0 = E_a(a_≤t)        # latent source = encoded PAST actions, not noise
â_{>t} = D_a(z_1)
c   = MLP(E_I(I_≤t))
L_FM = E || f_θ(z_τ,τ,c) − v_τ(z_τ,τ,c) ||²
L_AE = E || a_{>t} − D_a(E_a(a_{>t})) ||₁
L_IC = E || ẑ_1 − E_a(a_{>t}) ||₁ + λ_0 E || D_a(ẑ_1) − a_{>t} ||₁
L_total = λ_1 L_FM + λ_2 L_AE + λ_3 L_IC
```

`ẑ_1` comes from actual ODE integration during training; the number of Euler
steps used is **not specified** — a genuine reproducibility gap. `L_IC` is
credited to VITA (Gao et al. 2026) and called "critical for avoiding latent space
collapse."

Architecture: ResNet-18 → condition `c`; CNN (kernel 5) compressing history →
`z_0`; AdaLN-MLP flow net in a 512-d latent space; residual MLP decoder.
n=8, m=8, λ_0=0.5, λ_1=1, λ_2=0.5, λ_3=1, batch 32. Autoencoder trained jointly.

## 3. Source distribution — the central point

**Not Gaussian.** A data-dependent deterministic latent embedding of the
previously executed action chunk. Crucially `a_≤t` is *executed* actions inferred
from proprioception, "rather than previously commanded actions, accounting for
imperfect low-level tracking" — needs no extra sensing.

History "can also be corrupted with subtle noise to introduce stochasticity and
preserve a certain degree of multimodality" (σ=0.02 in the multimodality
appendix; 0.1 STD in the initial-state-uncertainty experiment).

Mechanism: adjacent action chunks are physically similar, so `z_0` and `z_1` are
already close and transport is near-rectilinear (t-SNE, Fig. 8).

## 4. Training or inference? — **BOTH.** Requires retraining. Not a drop-in trick.

## 5. Benchmarks & results

RoboVerse sim (Close Box, Pick Cube, Stack Cube, Open Drawer, Pick-Place Bowl),
100 demos, 30 epochs; real Franka dual-view, 30 trajectories.

| method | steps | Close Box | Pick Cube | Stack Cube | Open Drawer | Pick-Place |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **A2A** | **6** | **92** | **92** | **86** | **92** | 90 |
| VITA | 6 | 88 | 88 | 80 | 90 | **92** |
| FM-DiT | 10 | 58 | 88 | 26 | 28 | 84 |
| DDPM-UNet | 100 | 72 | 60 | 36 | 64 | 66 |
| ACT | 1 | 82 | 86 | 32 | 80 | 60 |

Speed: 0.56 ms single-step, "up to 20× faster than vanilla diffusion." But the
speed win is **partly the MLP-in-latent-space architecture**, not only step
reduction — the paper says so explicitly. And the comparison is against
**undistilled** baselines only; consistency/shortcut/distillation methods are
mentioned in related work but **never benchmarked**, which materially weakens the
low-NFE claim.

Step sweep: "marginal gains diminish significantly beyond 4 steps." One-step
exceeds 90% only after ~32 training epochs — **one-step generation costs extra
training, it is not free.**

## 6. The ablation that matters most to us (Fig. 10)

Flow-latent vs **Reg**-latent (deterministic regression in the same latent
space): both reach high in-distribution success, but regression "fails to
generalize to unseen scenarios."

> **This is the paper's own evidence on H1.** In-distribution, generation is not
> necessary. For robustness/OOD, it is. That is a directly transferable and
> falsifiable prediction for our direct-action experiment.

Generalization (Table 2, visual randomization Levels 0–3): A2A 6-step
100/38/42/38; A2A 1-step 100/20/16/22; VITA 100/4/2/2. **All methods degrade
catastrophically** — A2A is relatively better but absolutely weak.

Missing: **no ablation isolating L_IC or L_AE.** Their necessity is asserted by
citation, not demonstrated.

Honest admission (§4.3.2): A2A is "indeed more sensitive to uncertainties within
the action history" than noise-initialized baselines. Injecting 0.1 STD noise
recovers much of the loss.

## 7. Stated limitations

"Limited benefit" for "discrete or switch-like action dimensions, such as binary
gripper open/close"; "requires manual tuning of several loss-weighting
coefficients"; sensitivity to action-history uncertainty; open question of "how
to optimally fuse clean historical data with Gaussian noise."

**Unaddressed anywhere:** cold-start at episode step 0 when no action history
exists. A real gap for adoption — our PandaPush rollouts hit it every episode.

# Concrete ideas applicable to EC-Diffuser

Ranked. Our setup: `x_t=(1−t)·noise+t·clean`, transformer over DLP tokens +
action token, H=5, action_dim=3, NFE=4, PandaPush.

### 1. Regression-vs-generation ablation (Fig. 10 template)
- **Fit: excellent** — directly targets Tal's H1, with a concrete falsifiable
  prediction (parity in-distribution, generation wins OOD).
- **Invasiveness: moderate** (deterministic head variant).
- **Compute: one training run.** **Confound risk: low-moderate** — needs an OOD
  eval to show anything, since in-distribution the paper predicts a tie.
- **This is the highest-value idea and it converges with our Stage C.**

### 2. Replace Gaussian source with previous executed action chunk
- **Fit: excellent algebraically** — our interpolant is identical; the change is
  literally `noise → E_a(a_prev)` at t=0. PandaPush deltas are smooth.
- **Invasiveness: moderate** — history buffer, action encoder, retraining.
- **Compute: one training run. Confound risk: HIGH.** A2A bundles four changes
  (new source, latent space, L_IC, MLP backbone) and its speed win is partly the
  MLP. Any adoption must ablate the source swap **alone**.
- **Blocked by cold-start**: no action history exists at episode step 0.
- **Resolution warning**: A2A's 6-step gain over VITA is 2–6 pp — at or inside
  our measured evaluator resolution (conservative RC95 ±4.30 pp). Underpowered
  by construction unless seeds are budgeted.

### 3. Noise injection into conditioning history (σ≈0.02–0.1)
- Only meaningful if idea 2 is adopted; trivial (one line); folds into that run.
- Do **not** tune σ on the same episodes used for the headline number.

### 4. Latent-space flow instead of raw-action flow — **RECOMMEND AGAINST**
- A2A's latent is 512-d for an 8-step chunk. Our action space is H=5 × dim 3 =
  **15 numbers**. Encoding 15 values into 512 dims to "simplify transport" is
  dubious; their gain likely comes from compressing a much larger chunk.
- High invasiveness, high compute (four λ coefficients), and the paper never
  ablates L_IC/L_AE individually. Worst effort-to-insight ratio.
- **Note this directly informs our Stage H** (§39–44, action VAE): A2A is
  evidence that the latent-action idea is *less* promising at our action
  dimensionality than at theirs.

### Novelty warning (MP1/OMP constraint)
A2A occupies much of the "non-Gaussian source for action flow" territory, and
**VITA (Gao et al. 2026) appears to have already established the latent-flow +
inference-consistency machinery** — A2A cites it for L_IC and treats it as its
closest baseline. **VITA was not audited.** It should be before positioning any
EC-Diffuser work as novel here. Our genuinely open angle looks like
**object-centric / DLP-conditioned** source design, which neither addresses.
